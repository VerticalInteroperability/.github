# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in vertical interoperability and the work of VECTOR.

## Ways to contribute

- **Suggest a resource:** a tool, standard, dataset, paper, training program or community you think belongs in the
  conversation. Open an issue with a link and a sentence on why it matters.
- **Share an implementation story:** what you tried, what worked, what didn't. These are the material the group learns
  from.
- **Improve the website:** typos, broken links, clearer wording. Small pull requests are welcome.
- **Join the conversation:** introduce yourself in an issue, or write to contact@verticalinteroperability.org.

## Before you open an issue or pull request

- Check whether something similar is already open.
- Keep issues to one topic each.
- For anything larger than a small fix, open an issue first so the group can agree on the approach.

## Pull requests

1. Fork the repository and create a branch for your change.
2. Keep the change focused, and describe what it does and why.
3. A maintainer will review it. Content that represents the group (definitions, positions, publications) is
   published only once the group has agreed to it.

## Neutrality and disclosure

VECTOR doesn't endorse products. If you suggest or discuss a tool you build, sell or are paid to promote, say so in the
issue or pull request. That's welcome; it just needs to be visible.

## Code of Conduct

Everyone participating is expected to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
