# Vertical Interoperability

### Data that keeps its meaning across the research lifecycle

Most interoperability work is about moving data across a boundary: between systems, institutions or partners.
**Vertical interoperability** is about time and the research lifecycle. Data is planned, collected, extracted, analyzed,
shared and reused, often years apart and by different people. At each step, the definitions, methodological choices and
decisions that shaped the data need to stay with it, or the next researcher can't understand it and reuse it with
confidence.

## VECTOR

**VECTOR** is a working group of researchers, clinicians, informaticians, data stewards and research software engineers
that formed at a 2026 workshop on vertical interoperability. The group works on the concepts, training and community
resources that make research data reusable across its whole lifecycle, and on bringing together academia, industry and
federal stakeholders around them.

## How we work

- **Open by default, once agreed.** Material appears here when the group has agreed to share it. Working drafts,
  grant planning and meeting discussions stay with the group until then.
- **Neutral.** VECTOR doesn't endorse products. Tools, standards and vendors are discussed on their merits, and members
  with a commercial interest in a tool say so.
- **No attribution of discussion.** Views shared in the group's meetings are not attributed to individuals here.
