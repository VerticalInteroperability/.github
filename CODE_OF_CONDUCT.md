# Code of Conduct

VECTOR brings together people from research, clinical practice, informatics, libraries, industry and government. We want
every one of them to be able to take part.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially across disciplines that use the same words differently.
- Give credit, and disclose commercial interests when you discuss a tool or product.
- Respect confidentiality. Views shared in group meetings are not attributed to individuals outside the group without
  their agreement, and unpublished work stays with the group until it's shared.
- Accept that decisions about the group's shared positions are made by the group.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information or unpublished work without permission.
- Using the group's spaces for sales pitches or undisclosed promotion.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **contact@verticalinteroperability.org**. Reports are handled privately by the organization's
maintainers, who may remove content, or limit or remove participation, as needed.

## Scope

This code applies in the organization's repositories, issues and discussions, and at events held in the group's name.
